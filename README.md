# Personal work intelligence systems services section

The engineering root of the 561 Group's work program and the seat of its
Red Cup Engineering division, **Odometer Work Systems** (decided
2026-09-05 under the decision law; `package.json` here is the brand
package), seated beneath
[personal-intelligence-systems-services-section](../../README.md)
alongside its
[personal-health-intelligence-systems-services-section](../personal-health-intelligence-systems-services-section/README.md)
sibling, whose charter this one mirrors for work instead of health.
Founded 2026-09-05 from the program carrier,
[561 Group Work Services Section](../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/561-group-work-services-section/README.md),
which names the divisions and keeps their custody thread whole.

Odometer Work Systems owns capabilities whose primary subject is a
composed intelligence system that observes, projects, or advises on one
person's work state — earnings, costs, opportunities — on the person's
behalf: the pure compute (net-hourly, mileage, tax sleeve, cliff
projections), the record schemas, the statement importers over farolfi,
the companion intelligence, and the assistant engine shared with EVE
Assist. It does not own the underlying ledger data's storage, nor the
tax advice itself; it owns the intelligence system that reasons over
that data on the person's behalf.
